the browser extension. The extension has no user accounts, advertising, or
analytics service. It processes information in your browser to provide its
features; the extension developer does not receive that information from the

## Sharing and external services

The extension does not send your settings, browsing activity, bookmarks, or
playback statistics to the developer. Information may reach third parties in
these cases:

- **Browser provider:** Settings are saved in browser extension sync storage.
  If browser sync is enabled, the browser provider may process or synchronize
  those settings under its own terms and privacy policy.
- **Settings font:** Inter is bundled with the extension and loaded locally;
  the settings page does not request font files from Google Fonts.
- **GitHub site-report draft:** If you choose **Report a site**, the extension
  opens a GitHub issue draft whose URL contains the current site origin and
  browser user agent. It excludes the page path, query string, and fragment.
  Opening the draft sends the request to GitHub; the extension does not submit
  the issue. GitHub processes the request under its own privacy policy, and
  GitHub receives any details you choose to submit.
- **Links you follow:** Browser stores, project, and sponsor links take you to
  third-party websites that process information under their own policies.

These providers may process information in countries outside the European
Economic Area. Their own privacy notices describe their processing and any
transfer mechanisms they rely on. The extension developer does not control
those providers' processing.

You can export settings to a JSON file. The file may contain your configured
settings and site rules; it is saved to a location you choose and is not sent
to the developer. Keep exported files secure and delete them when no longer
needed.

## Your GDPR rights

Where the GDPR applies, you may have the right to:

- request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data;
- request restriction of processing or object to processing based on
  legitimate interests;
- receive certain data in a portable format, where the legal conditions are
  met; and
- lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in
  particular in the EU country where you live, work, or believe an infringement
  occurred.

To exercise a right concerning processing for which the extension developer
is responsible, contact the controller using the details above. Because
extension data remains in your browser and is not sent to the developer, the
developer may not be able to identify, access, or delete a copy of it for you;
use the browser's extension-storage controls for that data. Requests about
Google Fonts, GitHub, or browser sync should be directed to the relevant
provider.

If processing relies on legitimate interests, you may object. The controller
will stop unless it demonstrates compelling legitimate grounds that override
your interests, rights, and freedoms, or needs the information to establish,
exercise, or defend legal claims.

## Data required to use the extension

No information needs to be provided directly to the developer to install or
use the extension. Browser access to tabs and supported web pages is needed
for playback-management features. If you do not grant or later revoke the
relevant browser permissions, some or all features may not work.
