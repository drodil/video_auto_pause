# Video Auto Pause

![Video Auto Pause](vap.png)

Video Auto Pause is a Chrome and Firefox extension that manages HTML video
playback as your attention moves between tabs and windows.

## Supported features

- Pause and resume videos when a tab or window loses or regains focus, when the
  computer locks, or when a video leaves the viewport.
- Prioritize the most visible video when multiple videos are on a page, and
  prioritize media in the active tab with one-source mode.
- Configure global and per-site behavior, playback speed, named profiles, and
  one-click viewing modes.
- Control playback with the popup, optional on-video controls, tab pinning, and
  configurable keyboard shortcuts.
- Automate playback with idle and quiet-hour rules, ad detection, autoplay-next
  controls, and a sleep timer.
- Opt in to audio-only playback control while allow-listing music domains to
  keep their audio playing.
- Export and import settings as JSON.

See the [feature guide](docs/features.md) for details and the
[player compatibility guide](docs/compatibility.md) for known limitations.
